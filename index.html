Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how information may be handled when you use our fan-made simulator.

Last updated: November 7, 2026
Independent fan-made project. The vT Wuthering Waves Universe Simulator is developed by vT Official. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially associated with Kuro Games.

1. About This Project — Important Disclaimer

The vT Wuthering Waves Universe Simulator is an independent, unofficial, fan-made project developed by vT Official.

This project is inspired by the universe, atmosphere, characters, and concepts of Wuthering Waves.

This project is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially associated with Kuro Games.

All intellectual property associated with Wuthering Waves, including its original characters, artwork, music, trademarks, names, and other game assets, belongs to Kuro Games and their respective rights holders.

The vT Official name, branding, and independently developed systems belong to their respective creators.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the simulator may process:

  • Discord User IDs for account identification.
  • Discord Server IDs for server-related functionality.
  • Usernames and display names.
  • Gameplay progress and saved character information.
  • Echo Credits and in-game currency balances.
  • Investigation results and exploration progress.
  • AI conversation prompts and contextual information.
  • Technical information required for security and troubleshooting.

We aim to collect only information necessary to operate and improve the simulator.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Maintain user accounts and gameplay progress.
  • Provide exploration and investigation features.
  • Manage Echo Credits and other gameplay systems.
  • Generate AI-powered responses and interactive stories.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve the simulator.

4. AI Services and Third-Party Providers

The simulator may use third-party AI providers to generate responses and support interactive gameplay.

When you use AI-powered features, relevant prompts and contextual information may be transmitted to the selected AI provider for processing.

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies, terms, and data-handling practices.

vT Official does not independently control the privacy practices of external AI providers.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access to internal systems and stored information is restricted according to operational requirements.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with:

  • AI providers required to operate AI features.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Technical service providers.
  • Authorities when disclosure is legally required.

We aim to limit information shared with third parties to what is necessary for the relevant service.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the simulator, maintain gameplay progress, address security concerns, and satisfy applicable obligations.

Users may contact vT Official to request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal information.

Some information may need to be retained temporarily for technical, security, or legal reasons.

8. User Responsibilities

Users should not submit sensitive personal information through ordinary gameplay commands or Discord messages.

This includes:

  • Passwords.
  • Payment card information.
  • Government identification documents.
  • Private authentication credentials.
  • Other sensitive personal information.

Users are responsible for keeping their Discord accounts and personal API credentials secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The simulator is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Users should not provide sensitive personal information unless necessary and appropriate for a supported service.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when new features, integrations, storage systems, or other changes affect how information is handled.

Updated versions will be published on this website with a revised Last Updated date.

Users should review the revised policy before continuing to use features affected by an update.

11. Contact and Support

For privacy-related questions, data requests, technical assistance, or concerns regarding the simulator, contact the vT Official team through our official support server.
